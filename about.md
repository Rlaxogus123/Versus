# Versus

Open the Versus button in the online menu to browse or create two-player rooms.
Private rooms require a four-digit code. The room host chooses the level through
Geometry Dash's normal search and View buttons.

Both players download the map and ready up before the host starts. Matches use
attempt, percentage, Practice, or sequential rules after a shared countdown.
Progress, pause state, forfeits, results, and spectator movement are synchronized.
The stats panel shows the last ten recorded matches.
